## Kitty's privacy notice

Kittyfive ([your name], [contact address]) runs this site. This page says
what it keeps about you, why, and for how long.

### If you just read the stories

Nothing that identifies you. When the landing story plays, your browser sends
one **anonymous technical report** (browser and version, screen size, whether
and how fast the story started, how far it got, the first few errors), so the
story can be fixed on phones we cannot test on. No IP address, cookies or
identifiers are stored with it, and reports are deleted after 30 days.

Like every website, our host (Vercel) sees your IP address when you load a
page; it is used briefly to limit abuse (too many requests) and is not kept
by us. The site's fonts come from Google Fonts.

### If you make an account

Your **email address** and a **display name** (optional), kept by Supabase
(our database and sign-in provider) so you can sign in. Your password is
stored only as a hash by Supabase. Delete your account yourself on
**Your account → Delete my account**; your story subscriptions go with it
(stories you made stay on the site unless you ask).

### If you get new chapters by email

Your **email address**, which pet's stories you get, whether you are
subscribed, invited or stopped, and when each chapter email was sent (the
provider's message id; never the email's contents). Emails are sent by
Resend. Every email has a one-click way to stop. If you stop, we keep your
address marked "stopped" so you are never invited again; [ask us to delete
it entirely].

If the pet's owner invited you, you get one email asking you to confirm.
Nothing else is sent unless you press Confirm.

### If you chat with Kitty in the store

What you type is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to write her reply, together
with the last few turns of the conversation. We do not store or log the
conversation. [Anthropic's commercial terms: prompts are not used to train
models.]

### If you use the try-on

The camera picture stays **on your phone**: the tracking that places the
merch runs in your browser. Its program files are downloaded from jsDelivr
and Google's model storage. A photo is only made when you press the shutter,
and it is yours to save or share; we do not receive it.

### If you buy something

Your name, email, phone number and delivery address (collected by Stripe at
checkout), what you ordered and its status, kept to fulfil and support the
order and for [six years, for accounts]. Payments are handled by Stripe (we
never see card numbers); printing and delivery by [Printful], who receive
your name and address. Order and shipping emails are sent by Resend.

### If you look after a pet on the site

The photos, words and clips you add to its stories, stored by Supabase and
shown publicly once you publish them (drafts are visible only to you). When
you ask for a chapter draft, your photos and words are sent to Anthropic
(Claude) to write it.

### Your browser's storage

The site keeps a few things in your browser, not on our servers: that you
are signed in, a hint that you are an admin, and (in the demo mode) pretend
edits. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

### Who else handles it

Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage; EU, Frankfurt),
Anthropic (chat and chapter drafts), Resend (email), Stripe (payments),
[Printful] (printing and delivery), Google Fonts, jsDelivr and Google's model
storage (try-on files).

### Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or object to
how it is used: [contact address]. You can also complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
