# assets-raw/tryon

Photos and short videos of a real person wearing the try-on, so the next
session can fit the cap and the hoodie to a real head and body instead of
posed test figures. **Never committed and never deployed**: only this README
is. They are used on this machine only, played into the test browser as its
camera, so the site's own tracking runs on them frame by frame.

Put yours in `felix/`. Phone videos straight from the camera roll are fine
(.mov or .mp4); photos too (.jpg, .heic).

## What helps most

| File | How | For |
|---|---|---|
| `01-selfie-turn.mov` | Front (selfie) camera at arm's length, face in the middle, 15 s: look straight, turn slowly to full profile left and back, then right, then look up and down | where the cap sits as the head turns |
| `02-selfie-real-cap.mov` | The same, **wearing any real baseball cap** | the truth to match: how big a cap is on you and where its brim sits |
| `03-upper-body.mov` | Phone propped at chest height about 2 m away (or someone filming), head to hips in frame, 15 s: arms by your sides, arms out, one arm up, hands in front of you, turn 45° each way | the hoodie and long-sleeve on shoulders, arms and hips |
| `04-upper-body-hoodie.mov` | The same, **wearing a plain hoodie** | the truth to match: where shoulders, cuffs and hem fall |
| `05-full-body.mov` | Stand back until your feet are in frame, 10 s | Kitty sitting on the floor beside you |

Stills work too if video is awkward: face front, three-quarter left and
right, profile left and right, looking up and down; then the same standing
back.

**Tips:** even light (not a bright window behind you), a plain top rather
than bold stripes, hair as you usually wear it.

The screenshots already here (`feedback-*.jpg`) are the try-on as it looked
on 23 Sep 2026; they show what to fix, but the overlay covers what the
tracker needs, so clean footage is still wanted.
